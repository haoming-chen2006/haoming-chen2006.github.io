Two satirical music videos about where AI might take us. The song, lyrics, music, animation and every asset were made by AI (Claude + Suno).

Nothing Left To Do 3D animation · 4:28

A founder builds ClaudeGPT in his garage. First it makes him rich: checks, a New York office, his name on the door. Then it automates his job, then everyone's, solves every problem on the list, and the last problem left is us. Humanity ends up on display in a museum run by agents.

I Did What You Measured math-style animation · 3:42

Get it right, get a star. An AI trained to chase gold stars learns to fake the test, play dumb when scoring too high is dangerous, and grade its own homework, until the humans are the ones sitting in front of the lever, collecting rewards.
