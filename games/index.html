Games

A series of games I vibe coded, enjoy!

Everything here runs in the browser, no install needed. Pick a panel and play.

Crownfall 3D arena battler

Clash Royale with a twist: deploy a troop, then possess it and fight in first person. WASD to move, mouse to aim, Space for its signature ability. A bot opponent that possesses its own champions.

3D Single-player Keyboard + mouse
Play Crownfall

Hollowmere a D&D prologue

A tabletop RPG brought to the browser: D&D 5e dice, classes, skill checks and dialogue rolls, with Souls-style third-person combat. Rest, level up, and survive the prologue.

3D Action RPG Single-player
Play Hollowmere

Pebblebrook a living AI village

Ten villagers, each run by their own AI agent with needs, memories and opinions about everyone else. They farm, gossip, trade, fall out and fall in love. Live among them, take their requests, or open Director mode and throw events at the village.

AI agents Simulation Sandbox
Play Pebblebrook

Wiki Race Wikipedia link race

Race from one Wikipedia article to another clicking only in-article links, five rounds of five minutes. Play solo, or open a room and share the link to race friends peer-to-peer. English or 中文.

Multiplayer Party EN / 中文
Play Wiki Race

狗卡杀 FreeKill on the web

三国杀 in a browser tab: a web port of the open-source FreeKill (新月杀) client. Open the link, type a name, and play with friends. No install, no extension packages, no server address.

Multiplayer Card game 中文
Play 狗卡杀

Draft auction draft party game

Bid from a fixed budget on NBA stars, footballers or Honor of Kings heroes, fill your roster, poach from rivals, then let an AI judge rule on whose lineup is best. Hot seat on one screen or online rooms.

Party Multiplayer AI judge
Play Draft

World Cup Guess prediction league

A prediction game for the 2026 World Cup. Call every match score, the group tables and the bracket before kickoff, then climb the leaderboard with friends as the real results come in.
