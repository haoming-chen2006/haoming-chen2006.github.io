This reminds me of the idea from lecture where the path points to the middle of the distribution. I think this happens because the noise level is 100%, so the input tells the model nothing about which digit it is, and the best it can do is point to the middle of the distribution of all the numbers. Here that middle is this blurry shape, a mix of all the digits together, which matches the average of the training images shown above. Epoch 1 and epoch 5 look the same because they are still pointing in the same direction.

Without the learning-rate scheduler

I removed the scheduler and decreased the learning rate from 1e-2 to 1e-3. My philosophy is that moving in small steps prevents overshooting the loss. If it moves in small steps for enough steps, even if it oscillates, it will still move toward a lower loss, just like the scheduler does. As a result, my loss curve fluctuates more, but it still generally goes lower.
