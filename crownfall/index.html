How to Play

Crownfall is a tower-defense duel with a twist: you can leave the throne, possess any troop you deploy, and fight in first person.

1

Command

Elixir refills over time. Press 1–4 or click a card, then click your half of the arena to deploy it. Troops march down their lane and attack what they meet.

2

Possess

Hover a troop and press F (or click it). Your soul flies into it and the camera drops into first person. Possessed troops are Soulbound: +25% health.

3

Fight

Click once to capture the mouse, then look around. WASD moves, hold click attacks at the crosshair, Space fires your signature ability, Shift dashes, V swaps to over-the-shoulder.

4

Summon & return

While possessed, cards deploy anywhere within 4 tiles of your champion, even deep in enemy territory. Every kill harvests elixir. Press E to return to the throne, or fight until you fall and return automatically.

Win — take more crowns in 3 minutes. Each Princess Tower is a crown; the King Tower wins outright.
Tempo — the last minute is double elixir. A tie goes to overtime: next tower wins.
Keys — P pause · M mute · Esc release the mouse · wheel zooms the commander view.
